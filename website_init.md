Mandatory compliance standards:

WCAG 2.1 Level AA minimum conformance across all pages
Perceivable: All non-text content has text alternatives. All video/audio has captions. Content is adaptable and distinguishable (sufficient color contrast ratios: 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text per SC 1.4.3).

Robust: Content is compatible with current and future assistive technologies. Valid, semantic HTML5. Proper ARIA labels where needed.
